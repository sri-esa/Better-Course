# StudyFlow: Student User Guide

StudyFlow turns unstructured educational YouTube playlists into structured, followable curricula with realistic weekly study deadlines.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Paste Your Playlist
1. Copy the URL of any public or unlisted YouTube study playlist (e.g. CS50, MIT OpenCourseWare, or specialized tutorials).
2. Paste the link into the **YouTube playlist link** field on the home page.

### 2. Set Your Study Commitment
- **Hours per week**: Enter the number of hours you can realistically study each week (e.g. 5, 8, 10, 15).
- **Target completion date (Optional)**: If you have an upcoming exam, interview, or deadline, enter your target completion date.

### 3. Generate Your Plan
Click **Create my plan**. StudyFlow will:
1. Fetch all video details, durations, and titles.
2. Group related videos into coherent topics using AI and topic clustering.
3. Apply difficulty multipliers so you don't underestimate note-taking and practice overhead.
4. Pack topics into structured weekly blocks with deadlines.

---

## Following Your Plan

### The Plan Dashboard
- **Overall Progress**: Shows total percentage completed based on actual effort hours, not just video count.
- **Weekly Schedule**: Broken down into 7-day blocks showing the planned topics, deadlines, and assigned videos.
- **Pacing Status**:
  - `On track`: You are keeping pace with your schedule.
  - `Ahead`: You are more than 10% ahead of schedule!
  - `Behind`: You are lagging behind your weekly targets.
  - `Complete`: All videos in your curriculum are finished!

### Checking Off Lessons
- Check the box next to each video as you finish watching and studying it.
- Click the play icon (▶) on any video to open it directly on YouTube.
- Use **Mark week done** to quickly complete all lessons for a finished week.

### Pacing Alerts and Re-planning
- **Feasibility Alert**: If your target date is too tight for your chosen weekly hours, StudyFlow suggests the minimum hours/week needed. Click **Apply** to adjust your pace instantly.
- **Behind Schedule?**: If you fall behind, click **Re-plan schedule**. StudyFlow will take your remaining uncompleted videos, analyze your historical speed, and generate a fresh schedule starting today.
